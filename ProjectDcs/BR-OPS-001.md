## BR-OPS-001: Operations Reporting Suite

### Purpose
Provide inventory, banking, and tax reporting for operations management.

### Scope
- Inventory history tracking
- Pick list generation
- Price change monitoring
- Bank reconciliation
- Tax reporting

### Stakeholders
- Warehouse Managers
- Purchasing
- Finance Team
- Tax Accountants

### Requirements
- FR-OPS-001-001: Inventory History Report
- FR-OPS-001-002: Pick List Report
- FR-OPS-001-003: Price Changes Since Report
- FR-OPS-001-004: Bank Daily Sweep Report
- FR-OPS-001-005: Tax Details (Cash Basis) Report
